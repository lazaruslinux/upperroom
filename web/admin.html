            Slow mode

            Word filter

            Banned words

            One entry per line, or separated by commas. A message containing any of them is refused and never reaches the room. Matching is whole word, so "ass" does not block "class", and common endings are already covered: "fuck" also catches fucking and fucked. This one applies to everybody, moderators and you included.
